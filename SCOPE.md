# Scope

## Mission (in scope)
- Rehearse The Data Warrior's Way against real engineering work: ground before build, simplify to one action, prove with evidence, win or refuse the decision.
- Companion skills and Forge factory for data engineers, analysts, and neighboring roles. The manuscript remains the doctrinal source; this repo must not be required to read the book.

## Product (in scope)
- Agent skills organized by five rings plus Forge (Tools) and Practice.
- Ground-gated forge flow: grill → spec → steps → red/green build → three reviews → human approve.
- The Forge software factory runtime (`forge` CLI, `forge/line`, `forge-line/jobs/`) with platform-neutral defaults. Real approval requires a recorded test execution and three build-bound reviews. Demo evidence is synthetic and cannot qualify a real job.
- Small demos and SDK modules the factory needs (discovery, engineering checks, local demo).

## Out of scope
- Foundation estate knowledge: Green Brick ops docs, workplace inventories, ADO Fabric deploy runbooks, rebuild ledgers, analytics SQL estates, or any copied `knowledge/platform` tree.
- Auto-approving production deployments.
- Claiming host behavioral success from CI structure checks alone.
- Copying Matt Pocock skill prose (process shape only).
- Invented Musashi quotations or unverified memoir details as skill text.
- Replacing manuscript editorial work; chapter prose stays in the manuscript repo.
- Treating Forge, agents, or platforms as the measure of success.
