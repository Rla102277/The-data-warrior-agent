# The Battle Map

Use when a task has multiple stages, handoffs, dependencies or sessions; **never require it for small work**.
Create or update a single `BATTLE_MAP.md` near the project's Scroll or at a user-chosen path. Keep it concise:
- Goal and current role (Deshi, Rōnin or Kensei)
- Current position and next action
- Completed and verified work with links to evidence
- Open questions, risks and blockers
- Decisions and changed assumptions
- A short route of upcoming milestones
Reconcile against actual files and tests on resumption. Don't duplicate the Scroll's full content or invent completion.

Follow `THE_WAY.md`. Use the host's native model and tools; no custom server, mandatory CLI or separate model key. Respect real permissions and never fabricate evidence.
