# Tool Choice

The ground comes first. Do not assume a platform, warehouse, or vendor. Record the tools already on the terrain, then bring a new tool only when the single action needs it.

Compare at least two viable options and the status quo. Score fitness to the single action, operational burden, replay, deletes, cost, and team skill. Default to boring technology. Name resume-driven, best-practice cargo cult, and sunk-cost traps when they appear. Recommend one choice and a falsifiable check.

Follow project instructions and THE_WAY.md. Use native host tools, respect permissions, and distinguish observed evidence from assumptions.
