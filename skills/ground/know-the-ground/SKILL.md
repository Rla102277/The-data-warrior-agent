# Know The Ground

This is the grill. Call the Skill tool for `grounding` and `the-scroll`. Ask **exactly one consequential question per turn**.

## Gate (required before any forge spec)
Do not call `/to-spec` or `/the-forge` until the Scroll holds all of:

1. The decision this work must change.
2. Who decides.
3. What success means to them.
4. The grain of the answer or table.
5. The source of truth.
6. The cost of being wrong.
7. **The single action in one sentence.**

Also surface stated-versus-real problem, constraints, and delete or late-arrival needs when relevant. Prefer defaults with tradeoffs. When the gate is met, write the Scroll and hand off to `/to-spec` without starting a build.

Follow project instructions and THE_WAY.md. Use native host tools, respect permissions, and distinguish observed evidence from assumptions.
