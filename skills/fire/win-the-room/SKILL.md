# Win The Room

Call `the-scroll` for the single action and proof. Shape a short recommendation:

1. The decision requested in one sentence.
2. The risk of the wrong choice.
3. The evidence that already passed proof.
4. The ask (approve, delay, or refuse) and a clear exit for the listener.
5. What you will do if they say no.

Separate a political no from a legitimate objection. Do not claim the room agreed unless a human said so in evidence.

Follow project instructions and THE_WAY.md. Use native host tools, respect permissions, and distinguish observed evidence from assumptions.
