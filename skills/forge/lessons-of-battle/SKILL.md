# Lessons Of Battle

This is the Chronicle. Compare objective to observed result. Identify root cause versus contributing conditions, evidence gaps, recovery cost and what to automate or test next. When the lesson is worth keeping, append `CHRONICLE.md` with the date, what was tried, what the evidence showed, and what changes next. Skip the entry when there is nothing to reuse. Do not turn retrospectives into mandatory paperwork or blame individuals.

Follow project instructions and the installed THE_WAY.md. Use native host tools, respect permissions, and distinguish observed evidence from assumptions.
