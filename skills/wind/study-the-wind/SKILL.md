# Study The Wind

Compare at least two viable approaches and the status quo when relevant. Evaluate correctness under failure, operational burden, latency, replay, governance, cost, reversibility and team capability. Explicitly compare CDC against timestamp watermarks where ingestion is involved. Compare storage and orchestration options the terrain can actually operate. Do not assume a vendor. Steelman the strongest alternative. Recommend a choice and a falsifiable validation test.

Follow project instructions and the installed THE_WAY.md. Use native host tools, respect permissions, and distinguish observed evidence from assumptions.
