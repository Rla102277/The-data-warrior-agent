# Adapt

Call `the-pivot` and `ship-the-eighty`. Ask one question if the single action itself changed; otherwise update the Scroll with a superseded decision.

- If the ground shifted: rewrite remaining steps on the Battle Map, keep completed proven steps, and return to `/the-forge` on the new current step.
- If the decision is already answered: ship the eighty percent, mark later steps deferred, and stop building.
- Do not restart from zero without evidence that proven steps are invalid.

Follow project instructions and THE_WAY.md. Use native host tools, respect permissions, and distinguish observed evidence from assumptions.
